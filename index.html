DAT points and density
Use Plot DAT density after importing a survey file.

Density uses the selected DAT types and Gaussian smoothing. Units: survey points/km². It measures survey-point concentration, not wind speed or damage severity; survey coverage affects the pattern.

Power 0.1–8: lower values blend more broadly; higher values emphasize local reports. More neighbors and a larger reach (5–500 km) give broader averaging. Interpolation still needs at least 3 nearby reports.
